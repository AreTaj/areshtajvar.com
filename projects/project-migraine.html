Health Tech Product Design

Migraine Navigator

A chronic symptom tracking and health analytics application designed with a low-friction, accessible interface.

Context & Problem

Tracking chronic conditions over time presents significant usability challenges, as self-reported symptom severity is inherently subjective and difficult to standardize.

During acute symptom episodes, light sensitivity, cognitive fatigue, and physical discomfort can make detailed screen interactions and complex data entry difficult.

Features & Functionality

Migraine Navigator provides a streamlined interface optimized for fast, tap-based logging during acute symptom episodes, eliminating tedious data entry and minimizing visual strain.

The application dynamically organizes triggers, symptoms, and treatments to surface a user's most relevant options, while tracking patterns over time to help users and healthcare providers better understand symptom episodes.

Key Highlights

  • Low-friction, tap-first logging interface designed specifically to reduce cognitive load and visual fatigue during episodes.
  • Intelligent interface customization that personalizes trigger, medication, and symptom tracking based on individual usage patterns.
  • Longitudinal health tracking tools to visualize symptom patterns and uncover potential episode correlations.
